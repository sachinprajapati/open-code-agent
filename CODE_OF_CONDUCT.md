# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the OpenCodeAgent community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Assume good intent, give useful feedback, and focus discussions on ideas and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, intimidation, publication of others' private information, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, reject, or edit contributions and comments that violate this Code of Conduct. Report concerns through the repository's private reporting mechanisms when available.
